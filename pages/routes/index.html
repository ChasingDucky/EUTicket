Iconic European Train Journeys

Discover Europe's most scenic, convenient, and popular railway routes - from high-speed city connections to legendary scenic journeys through the Alps.

High-Speed City Connections

🇫🇷🇬🇧 Paris - London

Duration: 2h15
Operator: Eurostar
Price from: €50

Travel under the English Channel via the Channel Tunnel. Passport control at departure station.

🇩🇪🇫🇷 Frankfurt - Paris

Duration: 3h50
Operator: ICE/TGV
Price from: €40

Direct high-speed connection through picturesque Rhine Valley.

🇳🇱🇫🇷 Amsterdam - Paris

Duration: 3h20
Operator: Thalys
Price from: €35

Popular route via Brussels. Book early for best prices.

🇩🇪🇦🇹 Munich - Vienna

Duration: 4h00
Operator: Railjet
Price from: €30

Comfortable journey through Bavarian and Austrian countryside.

🇮🇹🇮🇹 Rome - Milan

Duration: 3h00
Operator: Frecciarossa/Italo
Price from: €30

Italy's busiest high-speed route. Two competing operators mean competitive prices.

🇪🇸🇪🇸 Madrid - Barcelona

Duration: 2h30
Operator: Renfe AVE
Price from: €35

Spain's flagship high-speed route. Much faster than flying with check-in.

Scenic & Legendary Routes

🏔️ Glacier Express

Route: Zermatt - St. Moritz (Switzerland)
Duration: 8h
Price from: CHF 152 + reservation

The slowest express train in the world! Panoramic windows showcase Alpine beauty through 291 bridges and 91 tunnels.

🌄 Bernina Express

Route: Chur - Tirano (Switzerland-Italy)
Duration: 4h
Price from: CHF 62 + reservation

UNESCO World Heritage route. Crosses the Alps at 2,253m without tunnels or cog wheels.

🚂 Venice Simplon-Orient-Express

Route: London/Venice/Paris/etc.
Duration: Varies
Price from: £2,500+

Luxury 1920s-style train. The ultimate splurge for a once-in-a-lifetime experience.

🌊 Bergen Railway

Route: Oslo - Bergen (Norway)
Duration: 7h
Price from: NOK 299

Norway's most scenic route crossing the mountain plateau at 1,237m.

🏰 Rhine Valley

Route: Mainz - Koblenz (Germany)
Duration: 1h30
Price from: €20

Castles, vineyards, and the Lorelei rock. Take regional trains for best views.

🌅 Cinque Terre Coastal

Route: La Spezia - Levanto (Italy)
Duration: 30 min
Price from: €5

Mediterranean coastline with colorful villages. Get the Cinque Terre Card for unlimited trains.

Night Train Routes

Why Night Trains?

  • Save on accommodation costs
  • Wake up in a new city
  • Environmentally friendly
  • Romantic travel experience

💡 Night Train Tips

  • Book early: Sleeper compartments sell out quickly
  • Choose your accommodation: Seats (cheapest), couchettes (6-bed), sleepers (1-3 bed)
  • Bring essentials: Toiletries, eye mask, earplugs
  • Lock your compartment: Safety first
  • Breakfast available: Most night trains offer breakfast service

Cross-Border Routes

🇨🇿🇦🇹🇭🇺 Prague - Vienna - Budapest

The classic Central European triangle. Each leg takes 4-5 hours with beautiful countryside.

🇩🇪🇨🇭🇮🇹 Munich - Zurich - Milan

Alpine journey connecting three countries. Perfect for Switzerland stopover.

🇫🇷🇪🇸 Paris - Barcelona

High-speed through southern France. Direct connection or via Montpellier for coastal views.

🇳🇱🇧🇪🇫🇷 Amsterdam - Brussels - Paris

The Thalys corridor. One of Europe's busiest high-speed routes.

Classic Multi-Country Itineraries

2-Week Grand Tour

London → Paris → Amsterdam → Berlin → Prague → Vienna → Venice → Rome

  • Distance: ~3,500 km
  • Estimated cost: €400-600 (point-to-point advance) or €520 (Eurail pass 10 days)
  • Best time: April-October

1-Week Western Europe

Paris → Brussels → Amsterdam → Cologne → Frankfurt → Zurich → Paris

  • Distance: ~2,000 km
  • Estimated cost: €250-400
  • Perfect for first-time visitors

10-Day Mediterranean

Barcelona → Nice → Milan → Venice → Florence → Rome

  • Distance: ~2,200 km
  • Estimated cost: €300-450
  • Best time: May-June, September-October
