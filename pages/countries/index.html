🌍 Cross-Border Travel

Many European trains cross borders seamlessly. Popular international routes include:

  • Paris - Brussels - Amsterdam (Thalys)
  • Paris - London (Eurostar)
  • Munich - Venice
  • Barcelona - Paris
  • Vienna - Prague - Berlin

Make sure to check visa requirements and passport control procedures for international journeys.
