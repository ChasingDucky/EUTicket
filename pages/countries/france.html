🇫🇷 France Railway Travel Guide

Overview

France boasts one of Europe's most extensive and efficient high-speed rail networks. The TGV (Train à Grande Vitesse) connects major cities at speeds up to 320 km/h, making it faster and often more convenient than flying for many domestic routes.

Railway Operator

SNCF (Société Nationale des Chemins de fer Français)

  • Official Website: sncf.com
  • English booking site: sncf-connect.com
  • Customer Service: Available in English

Types of Trains

TGV (Train à Grande Vitesse)

  • High-speed trains (up to 320 km/h)
  • Connect major cities: Paris, Lyon, Marseille, Bordeaux, Strasbourg
  • Comfortable seating, power outlets, WiFi (on most trains)
  • Reservation required

TGV inOui

  • Premium TGV service
  • Enhanced comfort and services
  • Free WiFi and entertainment

OUIGO

  • Low-cost high-speed trains
  • Same TGV tracks and speeds
  • Limited luggage, extra fees for services
  • Book early for best prices (from €10)

Intercités

  • Medium-distance trains
  • Connect smaller cities not on TGV network
  • Slower but often cheaper

TER (Transport Express Régional)

  • Regional trains
  • Short distances within regions
  • No reservation required
  • Affordable prices

Ticket Types & Pricing

Standard Tickets

  • 2nd Class: Standard seating, comfortable and affordable
  • 1st Class: More spacious seats, quieter carriages, complimentary refreshments on some services

Fare Types

  • Prem's: Non-refundable, non-exchangeable, cheapest prices (book 2-4 months ahead)
  • Non-refundable: Mid-range prices, cannot be refunded
  • Semi-flex: Can be exchanged for a fee
  • Flexible: Fully refundable and exchangeable

Youth Discounts

  • Carte Jeune (12-27 years): €50/year for 30% discount on TGVs
  • Worth it if you're making 2-3 long trips

Major Train Stations

Paris Stations

Paris has 7 major train stations, each serving different regions:

  • Gare du Nord: North France, Belgium, UK (Eurostar), Netherlands
  • Gare de Lyon: Southeast France, Switzerland, Italy
  • Gare Montparnasse: West France (Brittany, Bordeaux)
  • Gare de l'Est: East France, Germany, Luxembourg
  • Gare Saint-Lazare: Normandy
  • Gare d'Austerlitz: Southwest France, Spain
  • Gare de Bercy: Some TGVs to southeast France

⚠️ Paris Station Tip

Make sure you know which Paris station your train departs from! Stations are spread across the city and it can take 30-60 minutes to get between them. Always check your ticket and arrive with plenty of time.

Booking Tips

💰 Money-Saving Tips

  • Book 2-4 months in advance for Prem's fares (from €15-25)
  • Consider OUIGO for budget travel (luggage restrictions apply)
  • Travel off-peak (avoid Friday evenings and Sunday afternoons)
  • Compare prices on different dates - prices vary significantly
  • If under 28, buy the Carte Jeune if making multiple trips

Station Facilities

  • Luggage Storage: Available at major stations (€5-10 per day)
  • WiFi: Free at most major stations
  • Restaurants: Wide variety from fast food to sit-down restaurants
  • Accessibility: Most stations have elevators and assistance services

Travel Tips

  • Validate tickets at yellow machines before boarding regional trains (TER)
  • TGV tickets don't need validation - they're for specific trains
  • Arrive 20-30 minutes early for TGV departures
  • Platform ("voie") is announced 20 minutes before departure
  • Look for your carriage number ("voiture") on platform signs
  • Seat reservations are mandatory on TGVs
  • Large luggage spaces available at carriage ends

International Connections

  • Eurostar: Paris - London (2h15) via Channel Tunnel
  • Thalys: Paris - Brussels - Amsterdam
  • TGV Lyria: Paris - Geneva/Zurich/Bern
  • Renfe-SNCF: Paris/Lyon - Barcelona
  • ICE/TGV: Paris - Frankfurt/Stuttgart

📱 Useful Apps

  • SNCF Connect: Official app for booking and managing tickets
  • Ouigo: Separate app for OUIGO bookings
  • Citymapper: Station navigation and metro connections in Paris

Useful Phrases

  • Ticket: Billet (pronounced: bee-yay)
  • Platform: Voie (pronounced: vwah)
  • Train: Train (same in French!)
  • Carriage: Voiture (pronounced: vwa-ture)
  • Seat: Place (pronounced: plahs)
  • Where is...?: Où est...? (pronounced: oo eh)
